Your assignment

Play naturally. Notice everything.

This alpha repairs bugs, softlocks, interface friction, clue logic, scoring, and dialogue while preserving the original mystery, artwork, puzzles, and character arcs. Reports from partial runs are welcome. A strange line, abrupt song, tiny hotspot, visual glitch, or moment of confusion can be every bit as useful as a crash.

Your own copy

Redux is a patch: you need your own DOS floppy copy of the game, version 1.000 (the CD edition will not work). Copy your installed game to a new directory, extract v0.09 into it, replace the existing files, and start a new game; saves from earlier alphas are not recommended. Redux's file names are uppercase, as on Sierra's disks; on Linux, uppercase any lowercase names in your copy first (for f in *; do mv -n -- "$f" "$(printf '%s' "$f" | tr a-z A-Z)"; done), so Redux's files replace Sierra's instead of sitting beside them.

New in v0.09

Redux now ships as a patch over your own floppy copy instead of a full game; the game itself is unchanged from v0.08, so v0.08 saves work. In v0.08, Dr. Smith no longer has Laura admiring an ankh he has lost, and he reacts when shown his ankh or the Dagger; Dr. Carrington reacts to the Dagger too. v0.07 brought Act 3 patrols for O'Riley, Dr. Carrington, Olympia, and Yvette, climbable vats, and a storeroom crash fix; earlier alphas brought Olympia's player-chosen passage, the Countess's phrasing, Act 3+ conversations, the final inquest, music, and many Act 1 and 2 details. None of these has had a full playthrough yet, so reports on them are especially welcome; test the patrols from a save made before the end of Act 2.

Reference setup

DOSBox-X is the reference environment for now. ScummVM reports are useful too, but please label them clearly.

Protect the evidence

Keep rotating saves. If something breaks, preserve the save that reproduces it and attach the matching LB2SG.* files.

Especially valuable tests

  • Take a legitimate route that a walkthrough does not recommend.
  • Restore before an act transition and choose a different action order.
  • Skip optional conversations and confirm the mystery still works.
  • Show evidence to every plausible character.
  • Test low, middle, and perfect-score act grades.
  • Mix correct and incorrect answers during the final inquest.
  • Report anything that feels off, even if it is not technically broken.
