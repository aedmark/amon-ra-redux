Dagger of Amon Ra Modernization
As the lead developer for the definitive edition patch of The Dagger of Aman Rah, my
primary focus will be transforming this "beautiful mess" into a compelling and coherent
mystery adventure game, ensuring that core progression is never accidentally locked and
that the complexity of the mystery is accessible to thorough players.
Here is the four-phase roadmap designed to address all bugs, inconsistencies, and pacing
issues highlighted in the video:

Developer Roadmap: The Dagger of Amon Ra
Modernization
Phase 1: Critical Stability and UI/UX Overhaul (Fixing
the Core Experience)
This phase addresses game-breaking bugs, stability issues, and the cumbersome user
interface that negatively impacts player engagement.

Feature

Fix/Action Required

User Interface
(UI) Efficiency

Reduce the interaction steps required for interrogation.
Currently requiring five clicks per question. Implement a modern
context-sensitive UI or 'double-click to ask' function to
streamline interrogations and alleviate the "excruciating" slog
of Act 2.

Save/Load
Functionality

Re-enable the ability to save and load the game on all screens,
particularly during the critical Act 5 chase sequence, where the
CD version inexplicably disables the menu, leading to player
frustration.

Invisible
Hotspots/Pixel
Hunts

Adjust the clickable hitboxes for small or obscure items (e.g.,
the skeleton key glint on the painting, the intercom buttons, the
poetry book) to match the visual representation, eliminating
frustrating pixel hunts.

Memory Check Error

Repair the code governing the "About" screen dialogue in Act 2 to
prevent the erroneous "There is not enough memory available right
now" message from displaying throughout the entire act.

Graphical &
Animation Issues

Correct animation glitches such as characters walking through
doors (Wolf/Olympia, Countess), character sprites scaling
improperly (Laura during chase, Laura approaching the headdress),
and characters walking over one another (Steve cutscene glitch).

critical plot dialogue is accessible, and activating unused content.

Phase 3: Gameplay, Puzzles, and Progression Logic
Refinement
This phase tackles the "dead man walking" scenarios and redesigns unfair puzzles and
progression locks that rely on precise, arbitrary actions.

Feature

Fix/Action Required

Dead Man Walking
Scenarios (Items)

Guarantee that items critical for Act 5 progression
(Wire Cutters for the pterodactyl wire, Snake Oil
Refill, Cheese for the rats) are either acquired
automatically, cannot be permanently lost, or are made
available near the point of use if missed earlier.

Snake Oil Refill
Mechanic

Revamp the snake oil use/refill system: Add visual
feedback (e.g., inventory icon update) when the bottle
is used or refilled. Prevent refilling a full bottle,
and make the refill container in the basement
significantly more conspicuous and accessible,
regardless of whether the player has picked up the
grapes.

Pacing and Act
Length

Review and potentially expand Act 4 to improve pacing
following the lengthy Act 2 and Act 3. Introduce more
meaningful progression triggers in Act 2 beyond the
mandatory 14 eavesdropping scenes.

Unfair
Deaths/Warnings

Implement a look or warning mechanic before Laura
automatically enters the dark secret passage in
Olympia's office, preventing unannounced instant death.

Arbitrary
Progression
Triggers

Simplify the sequence of events in Act 1 to acquire
the dirty taxi and dress, reducing the requirement for
talking to specific people in a specific order (e.g.,
talk to Ziggy, use baseball, visit docks).

Wander Mechanic
Functionality

Ensure characters halt their movement during
conversation, preventing them from disappearing middialogue. Implement a mechanism to ensure key
characters (like O'Reilly) remain accessible for
questioning longer in Act 3 and 4.

Feature

Fix/Action Required

Mystery
Accessibility
(Wattney Little)

Provide clearer, non-pixel-hunt clues to the existence
of Wattney Little and the contents of the police file.
This knowledge is essential to solving the mystery.

Evidence
Validity and Red
Herrings

Adjust the importance of "red herring" clues (Ankh
medallion, Evette's schedule, multiple footprints) or
integrate them logically into the final solution to
avoid penalizing players who follow the physical
evidence.

Hint Book
Contradictions

Correct the game's internal logic for the best ending
requirements to match the necessity of the evidence.
Specifically, ensure that non-progression items—like
the Red Hair and Bifocals—are the only required items
that can be missed, and clarify why the Police File
(Wattney Little) is not technically required despite
being narrative critical.

Quiz/Dagger
Contradiction

Fix the logic error that conflates failing to answer
theft questions correctly with magically losing the
Dagger of Aman Rah.

Grades and
Scoring System

Standardize the point divisor in the grading system
across all acts so that scores correctly reflect the
player's percentage of points possible. Fix the bug
that pulls the first F-grade message at the start of
the game.

Non-Essential
Quiz Questions

Clearly delineate between required and optional final
quiz questions. Eliminate points given for nonessential questions (Art Theft, High Priest) that "do
not affect your ending".
